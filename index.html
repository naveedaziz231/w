The Philosophy

Master of Materials

While traditional watchmakers relied purely on conventional metals, RADO broke the rules of Swiss tradition to forge revolutionary materials that outlast time itself.

Indestructible Hardness

Up to 1,900 Vickers hardness. Impervious to everyday friction, diamonds and sapphire are among the only minerals capable of leaving a trace on our ceramics.

Thermal Equilibrium

The Lineage

Milestones in Swiss Horology

Explore over a century of disruptive audacity from our founding in Lengnau to world-first high-tech material patents.

1917 The Genesis

Schlup & Co. Atelier in Lengnau

Brothers Fritz, Ernst, and Werner Schlup open a humble watchmaking workshop in their parents' home. By the end of WWII, the atelier was one of the largest producers of Swiss movements in the world.

1962 World First

The DiaStar: First Scratch-Proof Watch

RADO creates the legendary DiaStar 1 using tungsten carbide hardmetal and sapphire crystal. It stunned the global watch industry by remaining immaculate when subjected to drills and extreme abrasion.

1986 Ceramic Era

RADO Integral & High-Tech Ceramic

Launch of the RADO Integral, introducing high-tech ceramic into watch bracelet links. This landmark release forever cemented RADO as the undisputed Master of Materials.

1998 Plasma Physics

Patented Plasma Ceramic Discovery

RADO patents its scientific plasma oven process. Ceramic components are treated at 20,000°C gases, gaining an exquisite metallic luster without using any metallic elements.

2011 Monobloc Case

True Thinline & Monobloc Architecture

RADO unveils the ultra-slim True Thinline with an innovative monobloc ceramic case—injected as a single unified piece, eliminating the need for internal steel skeleton cores.

2021 Skeletonized Art

Captain Cook High-Tech Ceramic Skeleton

Uniting vintage dive heritage with cutting-edge skeleton calibres, featuring anti-magnetic Nivachron™ hairsprings encased in tinted sapphire dials and matte ceramic monobloc cases.

Haute Horlogerie

Swiss Micro-Engineering

Beyond visionary case materials, every RADO automatic calibre represents hundreds of hours of hand-finishing, precision calibration, and Swiss horological mastery.

Nivachron™ Hairspring

A titanium-based alloy hairspring impervious to magnetic fields generated by modern smartphones and laptops, ensuring pristine timing accuracy.

80-Hour Power Reserve

Extended Swiss automatic movements engineered to run seamlessly through the entire weekend without winding or manual intervention.

5-Position Regulation

Calibrated in five distinct physical orientations across temperature extremes to surpass international chronometer precision standards.
